Privacy policy

Budget for Android · Last updated 25 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Budget app ("Budget", "the app") handles, how it is used and the choices you have. Budget is developed by an independent developer ("we", "us"). If you have questions, contact us at mrpareekaditya2@gmail.com.

Summary

  • Your budgets and expenses are stored on your phone.
  • If you turn on Backup & sync, a copy is saved in a private app folder in your own Google Drive.
  • We do not run any servers that receive your data, and we cannot see it.
  • We do not sell or share your data, show ads, or use analytics or tracking.

Information the app stores on your device

Budget stores the information you enter so it can show it back to you:

  • Budgets: name, currency, optional spending limit, optional rating and the date created.
  • Expenses: amount, name, optional category, optional rating and the date added.
  • Categories you create.
  • App settings, such as whether backup is turned on and when the last backup ran.

This information stays on your device unless you turn on Backup & sync.

Google sign-in and Google Drive backup

Backup & sync is optional. If you turn it on, you sign in with your Google Account and Budget requests the following permissions:

  • Basic profile and email address (openid, email, profile): used only to show which Google Account your backups are saved to.
  • Google Drive app data folder (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata): used to create, read, update and delete a single backup file of your budget data in a hidden folder that only Budget can access.

Budget cannot see, open or change any other files in your Google Drive. The backup is sent directly from your phone to Google Drive over an encrypted connection, and is only read back by the app when you restore your data.

Use of Google user data

Budget's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular, Google user data is used only to provide the backup and restore feature, is not transferred to anyone else, is not used for advertising, and is not read by any person.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your information with anyone. The only third party involved is Google, which stores your backup file in your Google Account under Google's privacy policy.

Retention and deletion

  • On your phone: delete individual budgets or expenses in the app, or uninstall the app to remove everything.
  • In Google Drive: open Google Drive on the web, go to Settings → Manage apps, find Budget and choose Delete hidden app data.
  • Revoke access: sign out in the app, or remove Budget at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Security

Data on your phone is protected by Android's app sandbox. Backups are transferred over HTTPS and stored in your Google Account, protected by your Google sign-in.

Children

Budget is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
