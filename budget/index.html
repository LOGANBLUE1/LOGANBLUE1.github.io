Budget

Simple budgets and expense tracking for Android.

Budget helps you keep track of what you spend. Create a budget for a trip, a month or anything else, add expenses as you go, and see at a glance how much you have spent and how much is left.

Features

  • Create budgets with an optional spending limit and your choice of currency.
  • Add expenses with an amount, a name, an optional category and an optional rating.
  • See totals, averages and how much is left, or how far over budget you are.
  • Group spending with your own categories.
  • Optional backup and sync to your own Google Drive.

How Budget uses your Google Account

Signing in with Google is optional. Budget works fully offline without it. If you choose to turn on Backup & sync, Budget asks for permission to use a private app folder in your Google Drive (the drive.appdata scope), along with your basic profile and email address so it can show which account backups go to.

  • Budget stores one backup file of your budgets and expenses in that hidden app folder.
  • It cannot see, open or change any of your other Drive files.
  • The backup is only used to restore your data to your phone, for example on a new device.
  • Your data is never sold, shared or used for advertising.

Full details are in the privacy policy.

Removing your data

You can sign out in the app at any time. To delete the Drive backup, open Google Drive on the web, go to Settings → Manage apps, find Budget and choose Delete hidden app data. Uninstalling the app removes all data stored on your phone.
